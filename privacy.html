Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Multi-Email-Connector-Claude (“the App”) accesses, uses, stores and shares information, including data received from Google APIs.

1. What the App is

The App is a local connector (Model Context Protocol server) that you run on your own computer. It lets the Claude desktop app, made by Anthropic, work with one or more Gmail accounts that you choose to connect. The App has no hosted server of its own.

2. Data the App accesses

When you connect a Gmail account and grant permission, the App can access the following through the Gmail API using the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify scope:

  • your Gmail address
  • email messages and threads, including headers (sender, recipients, subject, date), body text and attachment names
  • labels, drafts and message status (for example read/unread)

The App accesses this data only when you, through Claude, ask it to perform an action such as a search, reading a message, or creating a draft.

3. How the data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the features you ask for:

  • searching and reading your email
  • composing drafts and sending email from the account you choose
  • organizing email (labels, read status, archive, Trash)

The data is not used for advertising, sold, or used to build user profiles. It is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

4. Storage

  • OAuth tokens: stored only on your own computer, in the App's data folder. They are never sent to the developer.
  • Email content: the App does not save email content to disk. It fetches messages from Google when needed and passes them to the Claude app on your computer.

5. Sharing

The developer of the App does not receive, collect or store any of your data. Data is shared only as follows:

  • Google: the App talks directly to Google's Gmail API to carry out your requests.
  • Anthropic (Claude): when you ask Claude to work with your email, the email data needed for that request is passed to Claude as part of your conversation. That data is handled under Anthropic's Privacy Policy and the terms of your Claude account.

Data is not transferred to anyone else, except where required by law.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Multi-Email-Connector-Claude's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Your choices and data deletion

  • Disconnect any account using the App's remove_account tool, or delete its token file from the App's data folder.
  • Revoke the App's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the App and deleting its folder removes all locally stored tokens.

8. Security

All communication with Google uses HTTPS. Tokens stay on your own device, so please keep your computer and its user account secure.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
